# Cyber Shield Portal

Build a landing page, login page, and register page for "Cyber Shield" — an AI-powered investigation support platform for digital evidence case management.

Visual theme:
Dark, futuristic cybersecurity aesthetic — deep navy/black background (#0a0118 to #1a0b2e gradient) with vibrant purple/violet neon accents (#7b2ff7, #a855f7). Use a subtle animated particle/matrix-style background (thin vertical falling data streams, low opacity) throughout all pages for a "digital forensics" feel.

Logo/Hero graphic (used on landing page hero and login page side panel):
A glowing violet holographic shield icon made of a fine digital grid/mesh pattern, with a soft particle glow emanating from its base like a holographic projection. Surround the shield with smaller glowing icon tiles arranged around it, connected by thin light traces: a network/graph icon (connected nodes), a magnifying-glass-over-folder icon (search/investigation), a fingerprint icon, a document/report icon, a people/team icon, a photo/image icon, and a 3D cube icon. Include a laptop silhouette at the bottom edge and a circular radar/scan effect beneath the shield. Overall feel: high-tech, protective, investigative — no human figures or imagery inside the shield itself, just the mesh/grid shield shape glowing on its own.

Landing Page:

Sticky top navbar: "Cyber Shield" logo (small shield icon + wordmark) on the left, nav links (Features, About, Contact) center, "Login" and "Get Started" buttons on the right

Hero section: large heading "AI-Powered Investigation Support Platform", subheading about consolidating case evidence and generating explainable AI-assisted reports for investigators, forensic officers, and supervisors, with the glowing shield hero graphic on the right and a "Get Started" CTA button on the left

Features section: 3-4 cards with icons summarizing key modules — Evidence Correlation, Explainable Lead Scoring, Forensic Report Integration, Knowledge Graph — each card with a glowing icon, title, and short description, subtle hover glow animation

"Human oversight" trust banner: a highlighted section stating that AI output is advisory only and every decision requires human review/approval — styled as a trust/ethics badge

Footer: minimal, dark, with links and "Synthetic data only — built for research and demonstration" note

Login Page:

Split-screen layout: left side is the login form on the dark background, right side shows the glowing shield graphic large and centered with soft ambient particle animation

Form fields: Email/Username, Password (with show/hide toggle), "Remember me" checkbox, "Forgot password?" link

Role selector (dropdown or tabs): Investigator / Forensic Officer / Supervisor / Admin

Primary button: "Login" (violet gradient, glowing hover effect)

Below form: "Don't have an account? Register" link

Subtle animated border glow around the form card

Register Page:

Same split-screen layout, form on left, shield graphic on right

Fields: Full Name, Email, Password, Confirm Password, Role selector (Investigator / Forensic Officer / Supervisor), Department/Unit (optional text field)

Terms checkbox: "I agree to the data handling and ethical use policy"

Primary button: "Create Account"

Below form: "Already have an account? Login" link

General styling notes:

Rounded corners (12-16px) on cards and inputs

Glassmorphism effect on form cards (subtle blur + transparency over the particle background)

Smooth fade/slide-in animations on page load

Fully responsive for mobile and desktop

Use a modern sans-serif font (e.g., Inter or Poppins)
